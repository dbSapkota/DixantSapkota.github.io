Research interests

My work lies at the intersection of control theory, power systems, and optimization. I am interested in the following questions:

  1. Can classical model order reduction methods be extended to address component heterogeneity, black-box dynamics, and increasingly blurred time-scale separations in modern power systems?
  2. Can we obtain accurate reduced-order models while preserving the underlying network topology and structural properties of the original system?
  3. Can optimization problems involving Lyapunov equality constraints be reformulated or solved more efficiently to improve scalability?
