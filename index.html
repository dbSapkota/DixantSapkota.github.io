About me

"Understanding complex systems."
"Building simpler models."

My research focuses on optimal model order reduction of networked systems, with applications in power systems.

I work at the intersection of control theory, power systems, and optimization. I am interested in building reduced models that preserve the structure and essential dynamics of the original system while making analysis and control more tractable.

I hold a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University.

Current focus

Structure-preserving reduction of second-order networked systems, semistable dynamics, and optimization with Lyapunov equality constraints.
